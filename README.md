# NÚ MAALE

**Maale Youth Skills, Enterprise and Livelihood Development Platform**

Amharic-first, Ethiopian Calendar-enabled youth skills, enterprise, employment and livelihood development platform for Maale, Ethiopia. Offline-first, culturally grounded and community-driven.

## Purpose

NÚ MAALE is the proposed digital support platform for the Maale Youth Excellency Initiative. It aims to connect young people with practical skills, vocational learning, mentorship, employment, apprenticeships, entrepreneurship, cooperatives, markets and sustainable livelihood pathways.

The platform is intended to support a wider human and institutional ecosystem—not replace trainers, mentors, community institutions, employers, buyers or extension services.

## Guiding philosophy

> Culture is our foundation. Knowledge is our capital. Innovation is our engine. Enterprise is our pathway. Prosperity is our outcome.

## Non-negotiable product direction

- **Amharic-first:** default language for navigation, onboarding, core workflows and user-facing content.
- **Ethiopian Calendar first-class:** E.C. date entry, display, validation, conversion and workflow support, with G.C. interoperability.
- **Maale language:** priority additional language, subject to native-speaker and cultural validation.
- **Offline-first:** designed for low-connectivity contexts and affordable Android devices, subject to field validation.
- **Community-centred:** human institutions, consent, safeguarding and local knowledge governance remain central.
