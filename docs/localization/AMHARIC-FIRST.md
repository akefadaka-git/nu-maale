# Amharic-First Localization Requirements

## Default experience

Amharic is the default language for navigation, onboarding, core workflows, help and user-facing communications. Amharic-first is a product requirement, not a later translation phase.

## Ethiopic script quality

The product must be assessed for:
- Correct Unicode storage and normalization.
- Reliable Ethiopic rendering across supported Android devices.
- Amharic keyboard input and text editing.
- Search, sorting and filtering that work with Ethiopic text.
- Line wrapping, font sizing and accessibility.
- Copy/paste, notifications, reports and exports.

## Language hierarchy

1. Amharic — default product language.
2. Maale — priority community language, subject to native-speaker validation.
3. English — additional institutional and technical language.

This hierarchy does not imply that translations already exist. Terminology, dialect, grammar, tone and cultural suitability require validation with competent speakers and community representatives.

## Translation governance

Maintain a controlled terminology register, identify reviewers, record approval status and avoid machine-only approval of culturally significant terms. User-generated and community-contributed content must retain its original language and attribution/consent metadata where appropriate.

## Acceptance evidence

Localization is not complete until representative workflows have been reviewed by speakers, rendered and tested on target devices, and verified in offline and online states.
