## 1. Executive finding

A targeted search of publicly accessible government sources and relevant Maale-specific research located:

1. Ethiopian Statistical Service (ESS) official population projections for July 2023 and July 2025, and an ESS publication page for the July 2026 projection.
2. An official South Ethiopia Regional State Office of the President page confirming South Omo Zone as one of the region's administrative zones and identifying Dimeka as its seat.
3. A Maale-specific research publication documenting six study kebele/locality names selected in consultation with the Maale Woreda Livestock and Fishery Resources Office.
4. A 2005 academic working paper documenting 12 historical government-named Maale villages and explicitly distinguishing those villages from smaller subdivisions called kebeles.

**Critical limitation:** No publicly accessible, authenticated, current Maale Woreda kebele register, official kebele code list, or complete current kebele-by-kebele population table was located in this search. Therefore, the official current kebele count and complete official kebele list remain UNRESOLVED.

This addendum completes the current public-source search and records its findings. It does not certify the current administrative register.

## 6. Conflicting counts and classification controls

Available sources refer to different geographic levels and periods:

- A secondary Maale study reports 23 rural kebeles and one urban kebele (24 total). This is not an authenticated current register.
- The 2005 academic paper describes 12 government-named villages and says those villages were subdivided into kebeles. This is a historical village-level description.
- A 2024 South Omo study describes 214 rural kebeles and 21 urban areas across the zone.
- A later South Omo study cites a South Omo Zone Health Department report of 268 kebeles, comprising 246 rural and 22 urban, across the zone.

The two South Omo zone-wide counts (235 and 268) differ. Neither establishes Maale's kebele count. Their difference requires source-date and administrative-boundary reconciliation with the responsible zonal authority.

Do not add or subtract these figures to estimate Maale kebeles. Do not assume that a village, gurda, settlement, kebele, health-post catchment or study site is interchangeable with another unit.
