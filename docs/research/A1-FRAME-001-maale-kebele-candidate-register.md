## 3. Verification objectives

The verification exercise must establish:

1. Whether each supplied name corresponds to a currently recognized kebele in Maale Woreda.
2. The official spelling and, where available, official administrative code or unique identifier.
3. Whether any entry is an alternative spelling, local settlement, sub-kebele, village, or other locality rather than a kebele.
4. Whether any kebele has been renamed, split, merged, transferred or otherwise changed administratively.
5. Whether the supplied list omits any currently recognized kebele.
6. The settlements and population coverage belonging to each verified kebele.
7. The reference date, source and custodian for each authoritative record.

## 4. Required authoritative records

Seek and reconcile, through appropriate official channels:

- A current Maale Woreda kebele register authenticated by the responsible woreda administration.
- The current administrative structure and kebele register held by the relevant South Omo zonal authority.
- The latest applicable Ethiopian Statistical Service (ESS) population estimates or census-derived administrative tables, including their reference dates and geographic definitions.
- Woreda population, household and settlement records, with the responsible office and source date recorded.
- Records of administrative boundary or naming changes, where applicable.

A secondary publication or an informal list may provide leads but cannot, by itself, establish the current official register.

## 5. Reconciliation register

For each supplied entry, the verification team must complete these fields before marking it verified:

| Woreda confirmation | Confirming office, record and date |
| Zonal confirmation | Confirming office, record and date, if available |
| ESS match | Matching administrative geography and population record, if available |
| Population reference date | Date or period to which the figure applies |
| Household count | Value, source and date, if available |
| Youth population | Age definition, value, source and date, if available |
| Boundary/name changes | Evidence and effective date |
| Discrepancy | Difference between records or names |
| Verification decision | VERIFIED, PARTIAL, UNRESOLVED, or NOT MATCHED |
| Evidence reference | Document title, issuer, date and secure storage reference |
| Reviewer and review date | Accountable reviewer and date |

Do not infer a unique official kebele from similar spellings alone. Possible variants should be retained as aliases until an authorized record resolves them.

## 6. Population and sampling-frame controls

A verified kebele list is necessary but not sufficient to establish a valid youth sampling frame.

Before calculating the final survey sample, the research design must also establish:

- The approved youth age range and eligibility definition.
- The relevant population reference date.
- Population counts by age and sex, where available.
- Household or individual sampling-unit definitions.
- The completeness and provenance of any household or population register.
- Geographic and demographic strata.
- Procedures for selecting eligible youth within sampled households.
- Nonresponse, absence and refusal treatment.
- Whether weights, design effects or subgroup oversampling are required.

If no reliable age-disaggregated register exists, document the limitation and develop a defensible alternative sampling approach for separate review. Do not manufacture kebele-level youth counts by applying assumed percentages to total population figures without clearly labeling and approving the estimation method.

## 7. Verification acceptance criteria

The sampling-frame register may be marked VERIFIED only when:

1. The responsible administrative authority confirms the current kebele register.
2. Every supplied entry has a documented reconciliation outcome.
3. Omissions and additional official kebeles have been investigated.
4. Alternative spellings and non-kebele localities are classified without deleting the original supplied names.
5. Administrative changes and effective dates are recorded.
6. Population figures are linked to their exact geographic definitions and reference dates.
7. Conflicting sources are reconciled or explicitly marked UNRESOLVED.
8. The evidence register identifies the record custodian and reviewer.
9. The Owner receives the verification report for review.

The final verified register must distinguish the original user-supplied list from the official reconciled list.
