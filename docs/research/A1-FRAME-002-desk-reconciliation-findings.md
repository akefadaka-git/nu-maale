## 1. Executive finding

The desk review identified current official ESS population-projection publications and historical official and secondary sources that can inform reconciliation. It did **not** obtain an authenticated current Maale Woreda administrative kebele register or a complete Maale kebele-level population table.

Accordingly:

- The 31 names in A1-FRAME-001 remain **user-supplied candidate entries**.
- No candidate kebele is certified as currently official solely on the evidence reviewed here.
- No complete or current kebele count is established.
- No current Maale youth population or youth sampling frame is established.
- The final sampling frame and sample size remain unapproved.

This document records source findings and discrepancies. It does not silently change the original candidate list.

## 2. Official population sources located

### 2.1 ESS 2026 Population Projection

The Ethiopian Statistical Service (ESS) website lists **Population Projection by Sex, Area and Density 2026**, created 13 October 2026 and last updated 30 October 2026. The publication page describes projections as of July 2026 and reports regional, zonal and woreda population breakdowns by sex and urban/rural residence.

Source: https://ess.gov.et/download/population-projection-by-sex-area-and-density-2026/

**Retrieval limitation:** The ESS publication landing page was located, but the underlying downloadable report could not be retrieved and inspected in this desk pass. Therefore, no Maale 2026 numerical value is transcribed here. The landing page is evidence that the publication exists, not evidence of a particular Maale value.

### 2.2 ESS mandate and data granularity

ESS states that its Population Statistics department generates population data by region, zone, woreda and kebele, disaggregated by sex and age, and provides settlement and household-related statistics.

Source: https://ess.gov.et/about-ess/departments/population-statistics-ceo/

This establishes ESS as a relevant authoritative source to approach for kebele-level age/sex data. It does not mean that the required Maale register or extract has already been obtained.

### 2.3 ESS July 2023 population projection

An ESS-hosted PDF, **Population of Zones and Weredas Projected as of July 2023**, reports the following for Male-Wereda under the South Omo Zone:

- Male: 57,314
- Female: 56,039
- Total: 113,353

Source: https://ess.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Population-of-Zones-and-Weredas-Projected-as-of-July-2023.pdf

**Classification:** Official historical projection, reference date July 2023. It is a woreda-level total, not a current 2026 count, not a kebele breakdown, and not an age-disaggregated youth population. It must not be used as the current sampling-frame denominator without an explicitly justified method and approval.

### 2.4 ESS 2025 and 2024 publications

ESS also lists population projections for 2025 and 2024. They should be retained as potential comparison sources, but the Maale row and any administrative-boundary comparability must be inspected before use.

- 2025: https://ess.gov.et/download/projected_population-2025-g-c/
- 2024: https://ess.gov.et/download/population-projection-2024/

## 3. Secondary sources and administrative-count discrepancy

### 3.1 South Omo regional study

A 2024 community-based cross-sectional study describes South Omo as comprising 10 woredas and Jinka city administration, with 214 rural kebeles and 21 urban areas. This is a zone-wide description, not a Maale-specific kebele count.

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11505120/

### 3.2 Maale milk value-chain study

A published study of milk and milk-product value addition describes Maale Woreda as having 23 rural kebeles and one urban kebele (24 total). This is a secondary research description; its reference date and administrative basis must be checked with the responsible government offices before treating it as current.

Source: https://www.mediresonline.org/article/assessment-of-traditional-way-of-value-addition-practices-on-processing-of-milk-and-milk-products-in-south-omo-zone

### 3.3 Livestock-feed study

A published Maale livestock-feed study identifies selected study locations using spellings including **Boshikoro**, **Woyiynsa**, **Tikiboko**, **Lemo kalendo**, **Gudo**, and **Ganto**. These are study-site references, not proof that every name is a current kebele or that the list is complete.

Source: https://www.omicsonline.org/open-access/assessment-study-on-livestock-feed-resource-feed-availability-and-production-constraints-in-maale-woreda-in-south-omo-zone-2332-2608-1000269-101428.html

## 6. Required official records and responsible custodians

The next desk-verification action is to obtain records from the relevant custodians:

1. **Maale Woreda Administration:** current signed/authorized kebele register; kebele codes; official spellings; settlements under each kebele; administrative changes and effective dates.
2. **Maale Woreda planning/statistics office:** current population and household records by kebele, with reference dates, methodology and source.
3. **South Omo zonal administration:** zonal confirmation of the current Maale administrative structure and any boundary or kebele changes.
4. **ESS Population Statistics:** July 2026 Maale woreda population row and the most recent available age-by-sex population data by kebele; geographic coding and methodology metadata.
5. **Relevant regional administration:** confirmation of the current regional/zonal administrative designation and official record custodianship.

Requests should seek copies or extracts of existing administrative records. No household enumeration, participant recruitment or field interviews are part of this desk stage.

## 7. Required reconciliation decisions

For each of the 31 candidate names, record:

- Exact candidate spelling from A1-FRAME-001.
- Official kebele name and unique code.
- Official administrative classification.
- Whether the candidate is a kebele, settlement, sub-kebele, alias or unresolved.
- Source office, record title, issue/reference date and custodian.
- Population and household totals with reference dates.
- Youth age/sex breakdown, if available.
- Boundary/name changes and effective dates.
- Match decision and reviewer.
- Unresolved discrepancies.

Potential omissions such as Woyiynsa must be tracked separately as **external-source leads**, not silently inserted into the Owner-supplied list.

## 8. Acceptance criteria for closing the desk verification

Desk verification can be recommended for closure only after:

1. The current administrative register is authenticated by the responsible authority.
2. All 31 supplied names have an explicit reconciliation outcome.
3. Additional names identified by official sources are recorded and assessed.
4. The total official kebele count is confirmed, with rural/urban classification where applicable.
5. ESS and local population figures are compared on matching geographic boundaries and reference dates.
6. Youth age/sex data availability is confirmed; if unavailable, the gap and alternative frame method are documented.
7. All conflicts and limitations are either reconciled or formally retained as unresolved.
8. The Owner reviews the evidence register and approves the verification outcome.
