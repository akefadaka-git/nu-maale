## 1. Reason for correction

A subsequent search of the South Ethiopia Regional State Office of the President's official contact page produced an email address rendered as **info@southethiopirspo.gov.et**, while the earlier A1-FRAME-004 v0.1 transcribed **info@southethiopiarspo.gov.et**. The official website domain and email domain as rendered are not identical, and the website page could not be independently opened in this pass. Search indexing may also have inconsistencies.

## 2. Corrected handling

- **Do not rely on either email spelling as a verified delivery address until confirmed directly.**
- The official contact page currently publishes telephone **0983989625** and physical address **Sodo, Wolaita, Ethiopia**. Use the phone to confirm the correct official email and the appropriate records-request routing office before sending.
- The ESS contact route remains **info@ess.gov.et**, telephone **+251-11553112** and **+251-11553011**, P.O. Box 1143, Churchill Road, Addis Ababa, as published on the ESS official contact page: https://ess.gov.et/contact-us/
- ESS 2026 population projection publication page: https://ess.gov.et/download/population-projection-by-sex-area-and-density-2026/

## 3. ESS 2026 publication status

The ESS publication page identifies *Population Projection by Sex, Area and Density 2026*, created 13 October 2026 and last updated 30 October 2026. It states that the July 2026 report provides regional, zonal and wereda-level figures by sex and urban/rural residence. The page's underlying PDF was not retrieved and inspected in this pass. Therefore, the exact Male-Wereda row remains NOT VERIFIED and no numerical 2026 figure is approved for use.

## 4. Authority and preservation

This erratum does not amend A1-INIT-001 v1.0, A1-FRAME-001 v0.1, A1-FRAME-002 v0.1 or A1-FRAME-003 v0.1. It does not change the 31 original kebele candidate entries. No official records request is represented as sent.

**Conclusion:** For the regional office, confirm the email by telephone before transmission; do not use the disputed email transcription. The ESS email and telephone details are confirmed on ESS's official contact page. The A1 official-record verification remains OPEN.
