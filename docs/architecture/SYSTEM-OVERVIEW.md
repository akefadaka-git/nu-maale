# System Overview — Proposed Architecture

**Stage:** A1 concept only  
**Status:** Not implemented; architecture remains subject to assessment

## System concept

NÚ MAALE is envisioned as an offline-first digital support platform connected to a community and institutional livelihood ecosystem. The system should enable youth to access learning, mentorship, work opportunities, enterprise support and verified market information while allowing authorized programme actors to coordinate and measure outcomes.

## Proposed logical capability domains

- Identity, profiles and consent.
- Learning content and skills progression.
- Mentorship and referrals.
- Employment and apprenticeship opportunities.
- Enterprise and cooperative support.
- Agriculture, beekeeping and livestock knowledge.
- Market actors and opportunity information.
- Community participation and knowledge governance.
- Monitoring, evaluation and verified impact.
- Administration, audit and reporting.

## Localization architecture

Amharic-first localization and E.C. calendar semantics must be architectural concerns from the beginning. G.C. interoperability, Maale language support, Unicode-safe storage/search and localization-aware notifications and exports must be designed rather than retrofitted.

## Offline-first concept

The system should be assessed for Android-first use, local data capture, offline learning access, queued changes, synchronization, conflict resolution and shared-device safeguards. Exact technologies and synchronization protocols have not been selected.

## Trust boundaries

Participant information, community knowledge, institutional records and market information require distinct access policies. Consent, data minimization, role-based access, audit trails, retention rules and secure synchronization must be specified before implementation.

## Architecture decision status

No framework, programming language, database, hosting provider, cloud service or deployment topology is approved by this document. Selection requires technical assessment, cost and sustainability analysis, local operating constraints and a separate implementation authorization.
