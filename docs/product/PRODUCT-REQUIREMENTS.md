# Product Requirements — NÚ MAALE

**Product name:** NÚ MAALE — Maale Youth Skills, Enterprise and Livelihood Development Platform  
**Short name:** NÚ  
**Stage:** A1 requirements baseline  
**Status:** Proposed; subject to A1 assessment and Owner approval

## Product intent

Connect Maale youth with practical skills, vocational learning, mentorship, employment, apprenticeships, entrepreneurship, cooperatives, markets, responsible finance and sustainable livelihood pathways. The platform is a support layer for a wider human and institutional ecosystem, not a standalone training application.

## Guiding philosophy

> Culture is our foundation. Knowledge is our capital. Innovation is our engine. Enterprise is our pathway. Prosperity is our outcome.

## Primary user and stakeholder groups

- Youth learners and job seekers.
- Youth entrepreneurs and cooperative members.
- Trainers, mentors and vocational institutions.
- Employers, buyers, suppliers and market actors.
- Community elders and cultural knowledge holders.
- Local institutions and programme coordinators.

## Proposed capability areas

- **NÚ Learn:** practical learning and vocational education.
- **NÚ Agri:** beekeeping, honey, agriculture and livestock-related learning and services.
- **NÚ Enterprise:** enterprise and cooperative records and business planning.
- **NÚ Work:** employment, apprenticeship and opportunity discovery.
- **NÚ Mentors:** mentorship and guidance.
- **NÚ Market:** buyer, supplier and market connections.
- **NÚ Community:** community participation and consent-based knowledge sharing.
- **NÚ Impact:** monitoring, outcome verification and accountability.

These are capability proposals, not implemented modules or a finalized release scope.

## Core product principles

1. Amharic-first user experience.
2. Ethiopian Calendar as a first-class calendar, with G.C. interoperability.
3. Maale language support developed with native-speaker validation.
4. Offline-first operation for low-connectivity settings.
5. Affordable Android devices as a key target, subject to field validation.
6. Human-led training, mentorship and institutional pathways.
7. Inclusive access across gender, disability, literacy and digital experience.
8. Privacy, consent, safeguarding and data minimization.
9. Evidence-based market and livelihood opportunity information.
10. Transparent, measurable outcomes.

## Non-functional requirements to assess

- Offline data capture, local persistence and safe synchronization.
- Conflict handling and clear sync status.
- Secure authentication, authorization and device/session management.
- Encryption in transit and at rest where applicable.
- Low-bandwidth performance and efficient storage.
- Ethiopic Unicode and accessible typography.
- Localization-aware date, number and text formatting.
- Backup, recovery, auditability and operational support.

## Out of scope at this stage

No production software, live financial transactions, lending, wallet, token, marketplace payments, AI automation, deployment, commercial operations or collection of real participant data is authorized by this A1 repository documentation task.

## Validation gates

Product scope must be validated against field evidence, user research, institutional capacity, legal obligations, data protection, safeguarding, market demand, affordability and operational sustainability before implementation is proposed.
