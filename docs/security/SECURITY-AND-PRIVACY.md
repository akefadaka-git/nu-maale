# Security, Privacy and Safeguarding Requirements

**Stage:** A1 requirements  
**Status:** Proposed; not an implementation or certification

## Principles

- Collect only data necessary for a defined programme purpose.
- Explain collection and use in understandable language and obtain appropriate consent.
- Apply least-privilege access and separation of duties.
- Protect data in transit, on devices and at rest.
- Maintain auditable access and administrative actions.
- Define retention, correction, export and deletion procedures.
- Design for shared-device risks and offline data exposure.
- Establish incident response, backup and recovery procedures.
- Protect minors and vulnerable participants through appropriate safeguarding controls.

## Sensitive community and participant information

Do not publish personal participant records, precise household locations, contact details or sensitive community knowledge in public repository issues, commits or documentation. Community knowledge must not be collected, reused or commercialized without appropriate authority, consent, attribution and benefit-sharing arrangements.

## Threat assessment topics

- Lost or shared Android devices.
- Unauthorized account access.
- Weak or reused credentials.
- Offline cache exposure.
- Synchronization conflicts and replay.
- Privilege escalation and insider misuse.
- Insecure exports and backups.
- Fraudulent employment or enterprise listings.
- Misuse of participant data or community knowledge.

## Required future controls

Before any real-data pilot, define a data inventory, lawful basis and consent process, access matrix, retention schedule, threat model, incident process, safeguarding protocol, security test plan and accountable data steward.

This document is not a legal opinion, security audit or claim of compliance with any law or standard.
